A letter for my girl

Read this whenever you need to remember where my heart stands

my Diana my mi niña bonita I need you to know that my love for you is not something that disappears when a day gets difficult or when your mind starts telling you that you are too much. You are not too much for me. I love your heart your voice your thoughts your softness your strength and every little detail that makes you you. I am not looking for a reason to leave and I am not keeping one foot out the door. I want to keep choosing you with patience honesty and actions you can trust. I cannot promise that every day will be perfect but I can promise to keep communicating to listen to you to work through the hard moments and to never make you feel that you have to earn your place beside me. I want a future with you. I want the ordinary mornings the ridiculous laughter the long conversations the quiet evenings and all the little memories we have not made yet. You are my person my favorite hello and the woman I hope to call my wife. I love you deeply and I am not letting go of what we are building.
always yours, your hamoodie

Why is she so pretty

A little gallery in words for the girl whose face I could never get tired of looking at.

I look at you and I see art. The way the soft pink in your hair frames your face feels like a sunset bleeding into the night. I love the striking, wild ink across your skin—those dark, bird-like shapes that make you look like something fierce and untamed, yet soft at the same time.

And then there are your eyes. The way they catch the light, looking right through me, holding a calmness mixed with a little bit of mischief. I could look at you for hours and still find new details to fall in love with.

I even love the tiny flash of metal from your lip ring when you smile or pout. Every little thing about your face is a masterpiece to me. You don't just look pretty, Diana. You look like the most beautiful, fascinating person in the world.

Why you are my everything

Not because I need you to be less than yourself — because I love the whole person you are

You are my favorite person to tell things to, the one I want beside me when life is beautiful and when it is difficult. I love your heart, your mind, your laugh, your voice, your little habits, your dreams, and the parts of you that you sometimes struggle to understand. I love you as a whole person, not as an idea of perfection.

I want to marry you because I do not only want the exciting chapters. I want the everyday chapters too. I want to learn your routines, make a home feel like ours, celebrate your victories, comfort you after hard days, and keep finding new ways to make you feel loved. I want a marriage built on friendship, honesty, laughter, loyalty, patience, and the freedom for both of us to keep growing.

And one day, if life lets me give you the moment I keep imagining, I want to take you to Rome, Italy. A sunset spilling gold over the city, a quiet beautiful place, no crowd around us, no one else in that moment — just you and me. I want to look into your eyes, hold your hands, and ask you to be my wife. Not because a perfect location makes the promise meaningful, but because you deserve a moment that feels like it belongs only to us.

I will keep choosing you, Diana. I cannot control every twist life brings, but I can choose to be honest, faithful, patient, and present. I want to build that future with you one real day at a time.
